PDF Website: Beginner Guide  A simple step-by-step guide to using the site.  Step 1: Open the site and choose settings  •   Open your website link in a browser.  •   Choose your language: English, Urdu or Sindhi.  •   Choose light or dark theme.  Step 2: Sign in  •   Tap Sign in and log in with your GitHub account.  •   You need a free GitHub account. Create one at github.com if you do not have one.  Step 3: Upload a PDF  •   Tap Upload.  •   Choose From device and select your PDF file.  •   Tap upload and wait until it says the upload is done.  Step 4: Create a PDF on the site  •   Tap Upload, then Make with customization.  •   Type your text and choose page size and layout.  •   Add images if you want, then save it as a PDF.  Step 5: View, listen, download and share  •   Open any PDF from the list.  •   Voice: tap the listen button to hear it read aloud, or play a recorded voice.  •   Download: saves the PDF to your phone.  •   Get link: copies the link so you can send it on WhatsApp.  Step 6: Check that it works  •   Open the site in another browser or on another phone.  •   Confirm your uploaded PDF shows there.  Need video help?  Search YouTube for: how to sign in with GitHub, and how to use GitHub Pages.  Note: button names on your live page may differ slightly from this guide.
